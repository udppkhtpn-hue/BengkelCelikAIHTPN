Before you upload

Sign in with your DELIMa account only.

  • Is this material mine, or lawfully mine to use?
  • Is the notebook private to me?
  • Would the original author object?

How your teacher marks these

  • Marks reward verification, your own voice, and honest declaration — not fluent text.
  • You may be asked to explain a passage of your own work aloud.
  • An honest declaration never costs you marks.

For the facilitator

  • Introduce this pack after Module 4, once the protocol has been taught.
  • Assignments 1–5 are formative; Assignment 6 carries real weight.
  • For Assignment 6, write the summary yourself and plant one subtle error for students to catch.
  • Do not use AI-detection software to accuse a student.
Use AI to understand a source — never to produce the work being measured.
